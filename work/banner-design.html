Context

Project Overview

This project presents a selection of promotional banner systems created for digital campaigns. Each concept was developed to maintain a strong visual identity while adapting effectively to different placements and aspect ratios.

Purpose

Design Goal

To create campaign visuals that remain recognizable and balanced across horizontal, vertical, square and mobile formats without losing hierarchy, clarity or product focus.

Approach

Design Focus

Campaign consistency, visual hierarchy, product presentation and multi-format adaptation.

04The system · Direct comparison

Multi-Format Adaptations

A closer look at how each campaign responds to its canvas: text hierarchy, product placement, image framing, calls to action and spacing.

Monthly OfferOne idea → three adaptations

Message beside the products → products above the offer → centered headline over a tall product scene. The outlined CTA is retained in desktop and app; the vertical artwork focuses on the message and product.

Sign Up OfferOne idea → three adaptations

Side-by-side model and copy → image-first compact stack → copy-first vertical layout. The original compositions preserve product recognition while shifting text width, image framing and the space around each element.
