01Context

Project Overview

This case study presents two promotional product landing pages created for the Dr. C. Tuna range. Both pages were designed to communicate product value through strong beauty visuals, clear hierarchy and structured content, while maintaining a polished and product-focused aesthetic.

Design Goal

To create landing pages that feel visually engaging and commercially effective, balancing promotional storytelling with clarity, readability and product emphasis.

Design Focus

Visual hierarchy, product presentation, editorial composition and conversion-oriented layout.

Dr. C. Tuna — Age Reversist
